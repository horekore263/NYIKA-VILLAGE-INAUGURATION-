Our Village

Nyika Village: resources, enterprise, people and ambition.

Nyika is not starting from zero. It already has people, productive land, water, livestock, household enterprise, infrastructure and strong ties between families, the village and those connected to Nyika beyond its borders.

Nyika Village is a growing rural community of more than 100 households and over 250 adults, alongside a significant population of infants, children and young people. Electricity, expanding internet access and two perennial rivers provide an important foundation for a more connected, healthy and productive rural future.

100+ households forming the village community
250+ adults alongside children, youth and infants
2 perennial rivers supporting productive activity
Power electricity connectivity across the village
Internet expanding digital connectivity and rural WiFi
01 • PRODUCTIVE VILLAGE

Agriculture and household enterprise

Village livelihoods span beef production, cattle, river-front gardening, fish farming, beekeeping, broilers, egg production and other household-based agricultural projects.

02 • NATURAL ENDOWMENT

Land, water, flora and fauna

Nyika is endowed with productive land, perennial water systems, indigenous vegetation and a rich rural environment. Development should unlock opportunity while protecting the natural assets that make the village valuable.

03 • HEALTH & WELLBEING

Healthy people, stronger households

Village development depends on health, prevention, wellbeing and access to trusted health information and services. Community-based health and wellness outreach can support awareness, early intervention, maternal and child wellbeing, prevention and connections to formal health services. Health must therefore grow alongside education, infrastructure, agriculture and livelihoods.

04 • DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

Modernise without losing what matters

The goal is not to turn rural life into an urban imitation. It is to build a modern, sustainable and connected rural community in which infrastructure, knowledge and institutions strengthen local livelihoods while preserving community identity.

One Village, Near and Far

The Nyika diaspora remains part of the village.

Nyika's community extends beyond its physical boundaries. Family members, friends and people connected to the village in Harare, elsewhere in Zimbabwe and across the diaspora can contribute skills, ideas, networks, equipment, professional expertise and resources. The principle is simple: development remains locally prioritised and community-led, while the wider Nyika network helps accelerate practical, sustainable projects.

Directions

Travelling from Harare to Tawanda Kaseke Homestead.

The venue is at Tawanda Kaseke Homestead, Nyika Village, Goromonzi District. Use Google Maps for the final approach or follow the simple route below.

  1. Travel from Harare along Mutare Road through Ruwa.
  2. Approximately 5 km after Ruwa, turn LEFT at the Goromonzi turn-off.
  3. Continue for approximately 11 km towards Goromonzi.
  4. At Goromonzi Police, turn RIGHT and proceed down towards Kasema.
  5. Continue towards Rusike Shops.
  6. After passing Rusike Shops, look out for the beacon and turn RIGHT.
  7. From there, follow the Google Maps location for the final approach to Tawanda Kaseke Homestead.
  8. If uncertain, return to Rusike Shops and ask for Tawanda Kaseke Homestead in Nyika Village.
