Our Village

Nyika Village: resources, enterprise, people and ambition.

Nyika is not starting from zero. It already has people, productive land, water, livestock, household enterprise, infrastructure and a strong connection between families and their village.

Nyika Village is a growing rural community of more than 100 households and over 250 adults, alongside a significant population of infants, children and young people. Electricity, expanding internet access and two perennial rivers provide an important foundation for a more connected, healthy and productive rural future.

100+ households forming the village community
250+ adults alongside children, youth and infants
2 perennial rivers supporting productive activity
Power electricity connectivity across the village
Internet expanding digital connectivity and rural WiFi
01 • PRODUCTIVE VILLAGE

Agriculture and household enterprise

Village livelihoods span beef production, cattle, river-front gardening, fish farming, beekeeping, broilers, egg production and other household-based agricultural projects.

02 • NATURAL ENDOWMENT

Land, water, flora and fauna

Nyika is endowed with productive land, perennial water systems, indigenous vegetation and a rich rural environment. Development should unlock opportunity while protecting the natural assets that make the village valuable.

03 • PEOPLE & WELLBEING

Healthy people, stronger households

Village development also depends on health, prevention, wellbeing and access to trusted information. Community-based health and wellness initiatives should grow alongside education, infrastructure and livelihoods.

04 • DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

Modernise without losing what matters

The goal is not to turn rural life into an urban imitation. It is to build a modern, sustainable and connected rural community in which infrastructure, knowledge and institutions strengthen local livelihoods.

Directions

Everything you need to reach Nyika, in one place.

Use Google Maps for the final approach or follow the route below from Harare.

  1. Take Mutare Road through Ruwa.
  2. About 5 km after Ruwa, turn right at the Goromonzi turn-off.
  3. Continue about 11 km to Goromonzi Police, then turn right toward Kasema.
  4. Continue toward Rusike Shops.
  5. After Rusike Shops, turn right at the beacon and follow GPS.
  6. If lost, go to Rusike Shops and ask for Tawanda Kaseke's place.
